"We don't need a website. We have Facebook."\
"Our customers come from Instagram."\
"Everything is on our Google Business Profile."

These are perfectly understandable arguments.

A business really can run without its own website.

The better question is:

> **Can a business afford not to have an online presence of its own?**

The answer depends on how it wins customers, what product or service it offers, and what role being online plays for it.

---

## A website is no longer just an online business card

A business website used to be little more than:

**About the company → services → phone number → email address**

Today it can do far more.

A modern website can be:

- a sales channel,
- an online shop,
- a quote request system,
- a booking system,
- a customer portal,
- a knowledge base,
- an online catalogue,
- a marketing tool,
- an automation platform,
- an AI assistant,
- or even a complete digital product.

So the question is not:

> "Do we need a website?"

It is:

> **"What business problem should our online presence solve?"**

---

## The first question: can people find you online?

Picture a simple situation.

Someone hears about your business from a friend.

What happens next?

Most likely, they search for your business name.

They check:

- what you do,
- which services you offer,
- where you are based,
- what references you have,
- what reviews you have received,
- how to get in touch with you.

According to Google, more than 80% of people search online for local information, for example with "near me" searches.

A Google Business Profile ties your business directly into Search and Maps results, and lets you measure things like website clicks, calls, direction requests and other interactions.

So your website is not necessarily the first place a customer meets you.

It may be the second.

But in many cases, that second step is exactly what helps them decide whether to get in touch.

---

## "But we have Facebook and Instagram"

Yes.

Social media can be extremely important for a business.

In Verizon's 2025 State of Small Business Survey of 600 US small and medium-sized businesses, 76% said social media has a positive impact on their business.

That does not necessarily mean social media replaces a website.

The two channels can play different roles.

```text
Social media   attention → following → connection → interest
Website        information → trust → decision → contact / purchase
```

Social media is excellent for reaching people.

Your own website can be the place you send them to.

---

## One of the biggest advantages of your own website: it is yours

A Facebook page or Instagram profile lives on someone else's platform.

You do not set the rules.

The algorithm can change.\
The layout can change.\
The platform's features can change.\
Access and reach can depend on how the platform works.

Your own website, on the other hand:

- runs on your own domain,
- represents your own brand,
- holds your own content,
- and is built the way you decide.

This does not mean social media is unnecessary.

Quite the opposite.

For many businesses, the strongest strategy is not:

> website OR social media

but:

> **website + social media + Google presence.**

---

## A website also builds trust

Imagine finding two similar service providers.

One only has a Facebook page.

The other has:

- its own website,
- a detailed description of its services,
- references,
- customer reviews,
- contact details,
- an introduction,
- frequently asked questions,
- and a clear way to request a quote.

That alone does not guarantee you will choose the second one.

But you have more information to make a decision.

In Google and Ipsos's 2024 study of more than 18,000 online shoppers across 18 markets (The Relevance Factor), 79% said they feel more confident when they have done the necessary research, and 75% cross-check multiple sources to see whether the information they find is true.

Your website can be one part of that research that you own and control.

---

## When might you not need a website?

Not every business needs a complex website.

For a new business, for example:

- the marketing budget may be very small,
- customers may come mainly through personal recommendations,
- social media may bring in most of the leads,
- a Google Business Profile may provide enough basic information,
- and there may be no need to sell online yet.

In that case, a simple online presence can be enough.

You do not have to build an expensive digital platform on day one.

---

## When does a website really start to matter?

It is worth taking your own website seriously if:

**✓** you want to win new customers from Google\
**✓** you want to show your references\
**✓** you want to describe your services in detail\
**✓** you want online quote requests\
**✓** you want to launch an online shop\
**✓** you want to automate bookings\
**✓** you want to build content marketing or SEO\
**✓** you want to generate leads from advertising\
**✓** you want to measure where your customers come from\
**✓** you want to build your own digital platform\
**✓** you want your business to have a professional online presence

---

## Not every website has to be complicated

This matters just as much as whether you need a website at all.

```text
Local service provider   a five-page site introducing the business
Restaurant               menu + booking + Google Maps + contact
Consultant               services + references + blog + contact
Online shop              product catalogue + basket + payment + shipping
Larger company           website + customer portal + integrations + admin system + automation
```

So the size of a website is set by the business goal, not by the size of the business.

---

## A website can be a sales system

A well-built site does not just say:

> "We are here."

It also says:

> "This is what we offer, this is what you may need, this is how it works, these are our results, and this is how to get in touch."

A well-designed journey, for example:

```text
Google search
      ↓
Website
      ↓
Service
      ↓
Reference
      ↓
Trust
      ↓
Quote request
      ↓
Customer
```

At that point, the website is no longer a cost.

It is a tool in the sales process.

---

## And what if you already have a website?

The next question is no longer:

> "Do we have a website?"

But:

> **"Is our website working?"**

There is a difference.

A website can be technically online while:

- it is slow,
- it is hard to use on mobile,
- it does not show up well on Google,
- the design is dated,
- information is hard to find,
- there is no clear call to action,
- nothing is measured,
- it does not generate leads.

In that case, you do not necessarily need a new website.

The existing one may just need optimising.

---

## You can build a business without a website

This matters.

A website is not a mandatory accessory.

A business can run on:

- Facebook,
- Instagram,
- TikTok,
- a Google Business Profile,
- marketplaces,
- recommendations,
- personal selling,
- or a combination of these.

The question is whether those channels serve your business goals well.

If they do, you do not need to build a big system right away.

But if you want to reach new customers, sell online, automate, or build a long-term digital presence, your own website can become a serious tool.

---

## A good website is not a goal. It is a tool.

This may be the most important idea.

You do not need a website because:

> "Every company needs one."

You need one because it helps you reach certain business goals more effectively.

The goal might be:

- more leads,
- more sales,
- less admin,
- online booking,
- a better customer experience,
- a stronger brand,
- a better presence on Google,
- or an entirely new digital business model.

---

## So, does your business need a website?

Maybe yes.

Maybe not yet.

But rather than asking:

> "Do I need a website?"

it is worth asking:

> **"How do I win customers today, and how do I want to win them a year from now?"**

Once you have that answer, it is much easier to decide whether a simple introductory site is enough, whether you need an online shop, or whether a fully custom digital system would serve you better.

**A good website is not built so that you have a website.**

**It is built to help your business.**
