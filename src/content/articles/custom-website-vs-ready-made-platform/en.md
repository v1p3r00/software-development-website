When you start a new website today, there are plenty of options to choose from.

**Wix, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or a fully custom build?**

The answer is not simply which one is "better". The right choice depends on what you want to achieve, which features you need, and how your business is going to grow.

## Ready-made platforms: a fast start and a lower entry cost

The big advantage of a ready-made platform is that you do not have to build everything from scratch.

With a site built on **WordPress**, **Wix**, **Webflow** or **Shopify**, you can be up and running in a short time.

They can be an especially good choice if, for example:

- you need a site that introduces your business,
- you want a portfolio,
- you are starting a blog,
- you are building a simple online shop,
- you want to get to market quickly,
- you do not need complex custom features.

### For example

A hairdresser, restaurant, consultant, photographer or small service business often has no need for months of custom development.

A well-built ready-made system can do the job perfectly well.

---

## When does a ready-made platform stop being enough?

The problems usually start when the website is no longer just showing information.

For example, you want:

**the customer to register → pick a time slot → pay → automatically receive an invoice → while an administrator manages all of it from their own dashboard.**

Or:

**the shop to calculate custom prices → manage stock → receive data from an external system → process orders automatically.**

At that point, we are not necessarily building a "website" any more.

**We are building a complete digital system.**

---

## Custom website: when the system adapts to you

With a custom website, you do not have to bend the way your business works to fit an existing template.

Development starts from your business needs.

A custom system can include, for example:

- a React or Angular front end
- a Spring Boot or other back end
- its own database
- an admin interface
- user and permission management
- its own APIs
- payment integration
- invoicing integration
- CRM/ERP connections
- AI features
- a custom booking system
- a 3D configurator
- automated workflows

The system is built for exactly what your business needs.

---

## WordPress / Shopify or custom development?

You do not necessarily have to choose between "good" and "bad".

They are solutions to different problems.

|                          | Ready-made platform         | Custom development  |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Starting cost            | Usually lower               | Usually higher      |
| Development time         | Shorter                     | Longer              |
| Custom design            | More limited / extendable   | Fully custom        |
| Custom business logic    | Limited                     | Complete            |
| Integrations             | Depend on plugins/APIs      | Designed freely     |
| Own admin system         | Limited                     | ✓                   |
| Own back end             | —                           | ✓                   |
| Complex automation       | More limited                | ✓                   |
| Scalability              | Platform-dependent          | Own architecture    |
| Maintenance              | Platform + plugins          | Own system          |
| Fast MVP                 | ✓                           | —                   |
| Complex digital product  | —                           | ✓                   |

---

## A custom shop does not always mean a fully custom system

This is an important distinction.

For an online shop, you can use **Shopify**, for example, and have certain parts developed specifically for you.

In other cases, a fully custom system is justified:

```text
Front end      React / Angular
    ↓
Back end       Spring Boot
    ↓
Database       PostgreSQL / MariaDB
    ↓
Integrations   Payments • Invoicing • Shipping • CRM • ERP • AI
```

The right architecture is always decided by the business need.

---

## The big question: how much does it cost?

A ready-made platform usually costs less to get started.

A custom system, on the other hand, can be a bigger upfront investment.

But the starting price is not the only thing worth looking at.

It is also worth checking:

- how much the monthly platform fees are,
- how much the premium plugins cost,
- how much custom changes cost,
- how dependent you are on a single provider,
- how easily you can extend the system later.

A system that starts cheap will not necessarily be cheaper in the long run.

At the same time, custom development is not justified in every case.

**The goal is not to pick the most expensive technology.**

The goal is to pick the right one.

---

## When should you choose a ready-made platform?

A ready-made platform is probably right if:

**✓** you need a simple website\
**✓** you want to launch quickly\
**✓** your development budget is limited\
**✓** you need standard features\
**✓** you do not expect the system to grow significantly

---

## When is custom development worth considering?

Custom development can be justified if:

**✓** you have a specific business process\
**✓** several systems need to be connected\
**✓** you need your own admin system\
**✓** you are building a complex online shop\
**✓** you want to automate your workflows\
**✓** you want to integrate AI\
**✓** you want custom 3D or interactive features\
**✓** you want the website to grow into a platform later

---

## And there is a third option

Not every project has to be built entirely on a ready-made platform.

And not every project has to be built from scratch.

A **hybrid solution** works very well in many cases.

For example:

**Shopify + a custom front end + your own integrations**

or:

**WordPress + custom development + your own API**

or:

**A custom front end + ready-made payment and invoicing services**

That way, only the parts that really need it are built custom.

---

## A website can be more than an online business card

A modern website can be:

- **an online shop,**
- **a booking system,**
- **a customer portal,**
- **an admin system,**
- **an AI assistant,**
- **a 3D configurator,**
- **an automated business process**

or even a complete digital product.

That is why, before development starts, the question is not:

> "WordPress or React?"

But this:

> **"What are we trying to solve?"**

Then the right technology can be chosen.

---

## In summary

**Ready-made platform:** a faster and usually cheaper start.

**Custom development:** more freedom and custom business logic.

**Hybrid solution:** the advantages of both combined.

The right choice does not start with the name of a technology.

**It starts with the business problem.**
